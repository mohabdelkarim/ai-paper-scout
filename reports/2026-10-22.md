## 1. LongZero: Enhancing Long-Context Reasoning in LLMs from Zero Data

**Authors:** Zhennan Wan, Jianfei Chen

**TL;DR:** LongZero lets a large language model learn to reason over long, multi‑document contexts by self‑generating structured question‑answer pairs and expanding evidence, achieving better performance than RL trained on external data.

**Key Innovation:** A zero‑data RL framework that self‑writes compact evidence‑structured reasoning tasks, then expands them into long multi‑document contexts, and trains the model with GRPO to improve long‑context reasoning without external data.

**Why It Matters:** It allows engineers to build LLM‑based systems that can answer complex queries spanning many documents—such as legal case analysis or scientific literature reviews—without expensive annotation or proprietary corpora, accelerating deployment of multi‑document reasoning applications.

**Tags:** reinforcement-learning, zero-shot, long-context, self-supervised, large-language-models

**Link:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30132v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30132v1)

## 2. Think Harder Where It Matters: Item-Level Adaptive Recursion for Sequential Recommendation

**Authors:** Zhanshuo Cao, Jiakai Tang, Yuqi Zhou, Sunhao Dai, Jun Xu

**TL;DR:** LoopRec learns to dynamically decide how many processing layers each item in a user’s interaction sequence needs, allowing early exit for easy items and deeper reasoning for hard ones, improving accuracy while cutting computation.

**Key Innovation:** A lightweight binary gate at each recursion boundary that adaptively decides per-item whether to exit or continue, enabling item-level dynamic depth without auxiliary labels.

**Why It Matters:** Enables real‑time recommendation engines on resource‑constrained devices by reducing latency and energy consumption while maintaining or improving hit‑rate, making it practical for mobile or edge deployments.

**Tags:** sequential-recommender, adaptive-computation, item-level-adaptive-recursion, dynamic-depth, lightweight-gate

**Link:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30128v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30128v1)

## 3. PhysiCalWorld: In-Context Physics Calibration for World Models

**Authors:** Yunhao Luo, Wentao He, Yinpei Dai, Hyelin Nam, Hongze Fu, Joyce Chai, Nima Fazeli, Jeong Joon Park

**TL;DR:** PhysiCalWorld calibrates action‑conditioned world models to the specific physics of a scene by encoding a short exploratory interaction into a compact physics descriptor token, enabling accurate predictions without fine‑tuning or explicit physics labels.

**Key Innovation:** The core technical contribution is an in‑context physics calibration mechanism that learns a physics descriptor token from an exploratory clip and injects it into a latent world model, trained end‑to‑end with only latent prediction loss and no physics supervision.

**Why It Matters:** It allows robotic agents or simulation‑based planners to quickly adapt their predictive models to new physical environments—such as different object masses or surface frictions—without expensive fine‑tuning or physics simulation, thereby improving real‑world manipulation and navigation performance.

**Tags:** world-models, physics-calibration, in-context-learning, latent-representation, planning

**Link:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30125v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30125v1)
