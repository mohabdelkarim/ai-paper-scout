## 1. Ranking-Aware Prompt Optimization for Multimodal Clinical Diagnosis

**Authors:** Tian Xia, Minghao Liu, Yiqing Liang, Laixi Shi, Jiayun Wang

**TL;DR:** They replace accuracy‑based prompt selection in multimodal LLMs with a pairwise ranking approach that directly optimizes AUROC, yielding markedly better clinical diagnosis rankings.

**Key Innovation:** A pair‑level Pareto prompt evolution (Ranking‑PE) that uses a pairwise‑ordering matrix to compute empirical AUROC as the column average, enabling AUROC‑directed prompt search without extra model calls.

**Why It Matters:** By improving ranking quality for rare but critical conditions, the method enhances triage decisions in hospital settings, allowing clinicians to prioritize high‑risk patients more reliably than accuracy‑only systems.

**Tags:** prompt-optimization, auroc-optimization, multimodal-clinical, reflective-learning, pairwise-ranking

## 2. Semifactual Credit-Augmented Policy Optimization

**Authors:** Junshu Pan, Zhizhang Fu, Shulin Huang, Yiran Ding, Zifan Cheng, Wenqi Shao, Qiaosheng Zhang, Yue Zhang

**TL;DR:** SCAPO refines token‑level credit in RL‑based LLM reasoning by measuring semifactual stability, boosting accuracy on math and out‑of‑distribution tests without changing model weights.

**Key Innovation:** A causally‑inspired policy optimizer that assigns token advantages based on semifactual stability, reducing credit for unstable tokens during early training.

**Why It Matters:** By improving reasoning fidelity and generalization in large language models, SCAPO enables more reliable math problem solving, code debugging, and other precision‑critical tasks in production systems.

**Tags:** reinforcement-learning, large-language-models, policy-optimization, causal-inference, reasoning-accuracy

## 3. Removing Timing Shortcuts Improves Non-Invasive Brain-to-Text

**Authors:** Dulhan Jayalath, Oiwi Parker Jones

**TL;DR:** By preventing neural models from exploiting word-duration cues in overlapping brain‑activity windows, the authors achieve more accurate non‑invasive brain‑to‑text decoding.

**Key Innovation:** A simple change that processes each time window independently, eliminating the timing shortcut that allowed models to guess words from duration alone.

**Why It Matters:** It improves the reliability of non‑invasive speech‑brain interfaces, enabling clearer communication for people with speech disorders without invasive electrodes.

**Tags:** brain-computer-interface, brain-to-text, timing-shortcut, non-invasive, speech-decoding

**Link:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40359v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40359v1)
