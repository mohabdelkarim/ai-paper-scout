## 2. Decoupling Exploration from Optimization in RLVR

**Authors:** Saif Punjwani, Micah Goldblum

**TL;DR:** ExpDis decouples novelty-driven exploration from reward-optimization in RLVR, letting language models discover diverse reasoning strategies without sacrificing quality.

**Key Innovation:** A framework that alternates between novelty-boosted explorer policies and a distillation step into a student policy trained without novelty, enabling aggressive exploration without degradation.

**Why It Matters:** It allows engineers to train math‑solving language models that generate a wider variety of correct solutions, improving pass@k and overall robustness for applications such as automated theorem proving or code synthesis.

## 3. Long-WAM: Scaling the Context of World-Action Models

**Authors:** Wei Huang, Bohan Zhang, Chenzhi Liu, Isabella Liu, Shuai Yang, Weian Mao, Luozhou Wang, Yicheng Xiao, Weifeng Lin, Qixin Hu, Bryan Chu, Sifei Liu, Linxi Fan, Xiaojuan Qi, Song Han, Yukang Chen

**TL;DR:** Long-WAM extends causal world-action models to use up to 19‑second visual histories in real‑time robot control by leveraging autoregressive video pretraining and efficient streaming inference.

**Key Innovation:** A framework that preserves the history‑to‑future structure learned via autoregressive pretraining, enabling long‑context causal prediction without sacrificing real‑time performance through streaming observation encoding and asynchronous execution.

**Why It Matters:** It allows industrial or domestic robots to perform complex, long‑horizon manipulation tasks—such as dynamic cup stacking or assembly—more reliably by accessing extended visual histories, directly improving task success rates in real‑world deployments.

**Tags:** long-context, world-action-model, autoregressive-pretraining, real-time-robotics, video-model
