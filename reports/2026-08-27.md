## 1. VBVR-Pro: A Scalable and Verifiable Suite for Native Visual Reasoning

**Authors:** Junxiang Xu, Ruisi Wang, Fanyi Pu, Maijunxian Wang, Ran Ji, Tongxi Zhou, Chenyang Gu, Jing Zuo, Hongcan Xiao, Yimeng Geng, Wanqi Yin, Wei Chen, Oscar Qian, Zhengan Yan, Ziqi Huang, Haiwen Diao, Liang Pan, Bo Li, Xiangyu Fan, Dezhi Luo, Fengyuan Yu, Zehong Zhao, Qingying Gao, Tinghui Zhu, Yilan Zhang, Jingqi Tong, Pinyuan Feng, Zhengze Jiang, Letian Wang, Ziyu Guo, Renrui Zhang, Jieneng Chen, Sonia Joseph, Constantin Venhoff, Saman Motamed, Mengyue Yang, Chandra Sripada, Alan Yuille, Philip Torr, Lvmin Zhang, Vikash Kumar, Daniel Khashabi, Nikolaus Kriegeskorte, Raphaël Millière, Vincent C. Müller, Anyi Rao, Quan Wang, Ziwei Liu, Dahua Lin, Lei Yang, Hokin Deng, Zhongang Cai

**TL;DR:** VBVR‑Pro is a new closed‑loop benchmark that turns visual generation into scalable, verifiable reasoning tasks, providing 300 procedurally generated problems, deterministic reward scorers, and a platform for studying how image, video, and interleaved generators solve visual reasoning.

**Key Innovation:** The creation of a large, procedurally generated suite of native visual reasoning tasks with deterministic, task‑specific reward scorers that enable reliable reinforcement learning and controlled modality analysis.

**Why It Matters:** It allows engineers to train and evaluate generative models that can reason about visual state—useful for autonomous robots or video‑based AI that must plan and act directly from visual input without relying on language intermediaries.

**Tags:** native-visual-reasoning, verifiable-rewards, procedural-generation, closed-loop-testbed, video-generation

**Link:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26105v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26105v1)

## 2. A Visual Dependence-Aware Framework for Multimodal Unsupervised Continual Post-Training

**Authors:** Kaichen Li, Zhilin Zhu, Jianhao Huang, Zhengqin Lai, Baochen Xiong, Zibo Shao, Yaguang Song, Linhui Xiao, Xiaoshan Yang, Changsheng Xu

**TL;DR:** A framework that uses token‑level visual dependence to enable multimodal language models to learn continuously from unlabeled data while avoiding cross‑modal forgetting.

**Key Innovation:** The VDA framework introduces Visually Constrained Optimal Transport to preserve old visual dependencies and Visually Modulated Adaptation to focus learning on visually grounded tokens, jointly maintaining stability and plasticity in multimodal continual post‑training.

**Why It Matters:** It allows deployed multimodal models to adapt to new visual content streams—such as evolving product catalogs or changing user‑generated media—without labeled data, improving real‑time personalization and reducing annotation costs.

**Tags:** multimodal-continual-learning, unsupervised-post-training, visual-dependence, optimal-transport, catastrophic-forgetting

**Link:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26095v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26095v1)

## 3. MyoMechanix: Biomechanically-Grounded Compositional Skilled Activity Understanding and Coaching

**Authors:** Hao Yin, Paritosh Parmar, Lijun Gu, Lin Xu, Tianxiao Guo, Xiujin Liu, Tianyou Zheng, Yang Zhang, Weiwei Fu

**TL;DR:** MyoMechanix delivers the largest multimodal dataset and a structured knowledge graph for biomechanically grounded action quality assessment, enabling fine‑grained error attribution and feedback for fitness and rehabilitation tasks.

**Key Innovation:** A multimodal ecosystem that synchronizes RGB video, 3D pose, sEMG, and physiological signals with a Fitness Knowledge Graph, coupled with the CUBIST engine that decomposes actions into compositional ontological elements for precise error analysis and corrective guidance.

**Why It Matters:** Engineers can build video‑only coaching systems that automatically detect and correct weight‑lifting posture errors, eliminating the need for costly EMG wearables and improving safety and performance for athletes and patients.

**Tags:** multimodal-dataset, action-quality-assessment, biomechanics, knowledge-graph, ontological-reasoning

**Link:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26094v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26094v1)
