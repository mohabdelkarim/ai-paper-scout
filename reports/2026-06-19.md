# AI Paper Scout Report — 2026-06-19

## Selection Rationale

These three papers were selected due to their technical novelty, industry relevance, and practical importance. Optimal Deterministic Multicalibration and Omniprediction (index 2) presents a novel approach to multicalibration, a crucial property for trustworthy machine learning. Toward Calibrated Mixture-of-Experts Under Distribution Shift (index 6) addresses the important problem of calibration in mixture-of-experts models, which is essential for understanding and trusting reported probabilities. Efficient and Sound Probabilistic Verification for AI Agents (index 17) introduces a probabilistic verification framework for AI agents, which is critical for securing AI systems that operate in complex digital environments.

## 1. Optimal Deterministic Multicalibration and Omniprediction

**Authors:** Georgy Noarov, Aaron Roth

**TL;DR:** This paper presents a deterministic algorithm that achieves optimal sample complexity for multicalibration, a property crucial for trustworthy machine learning, resolving a long-standing open problem

**Key Innovation:** The development of a minimax-optimal multicalibration algorithm that outputs a deterministic predictor, eliminating the need for randomization

**Why It Matters:** The innovation enables optimal sample complexity for various downstream applications, including outcome indistinguishability, omniprediction, and panprediction, advancing the field of trustworthy machine learning

**Tags:** multicalibration, deterministic predictors, machine learning, trustworthy AI, sample complexity

**Link:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20557v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20557v1)

## 2. Toward Calibrated Mixture-of-Experts Under Distribution Shift

**Authors:** Gina Wong, Drew Prinster, Suchi Saria, Rama Chellappa, Anqi Liu

**TL;DR:** This paper investigates the calibration of mixture-of-experts models under distribution shift and proposes an adversarial reweighting method to improve their accuracy-calibration tradeoff

**Key Innovation:** The introduction of an adversarial reweighting approach that penalizes calibration errors of the routed aggregate under distribution shift

**Why It Matters:** The proposed method has practical implications for improving the reliability and trustworthiness of mixture-of-experts models in real-world applications where distribution shifts are common

**Tags:** calibration, mixture-of-experts, distribution_shift, ensemble_learning, adversarial_reweighting

**Link:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20544v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20544v1)

## 3. Efficient and Sound Probabilistic Verification for AI Agents

**Authors:** Alaia Solko-Breslin, Pramod Kaushik Mudrakarta, Mihai Christodorescu, Somesh Jha, Krishnamurthy Dj Dvijotham

**TL;DR:** This paper presents a framework for probabilistic verification of AI agents that operate in complex environments with ambiguous security policies, providing sound upper bounds on policy violation probabilities

**Key Innovation:** The use of distributionally robust optimization to compute sound upper bounds on the probability of policy violation, without requiring independence assumptions between predicates

**Why It Matters:** The approach enables more accurate and efficient verification of AI agents in real-world applications, improving the security-utility trade-off and providing rigorous bounds on policy violation probabilities

**Tags:** AI Security, Probabilistic Verification, Distributionally Robust Optimization, Datalog, Runtime Monitoring

**Link:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20510v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20510v1)
